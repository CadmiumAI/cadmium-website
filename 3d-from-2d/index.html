What is happening

Geometry first, then inference.

Intersect

A point belongs to the part only if all three views cover it. That single rule already recovers any prismatic part.

Infer

Outlines alone would make a shaft square. When the end view is round and the other two agree, sections are rounded.

Extract

A surface is pulled from the field, at sub-cell accuracy, in tens of milliseconds, fast enough to rebuild while you draw.

Check

The solid is projected back onto each view. Anything you drew that nothing in the solid explains shows up in red.

What you get

A solid you can use, and a check you can trust.

From your views

  • A solid rebuilt from three orthographic views
  • A pipe route from a plan and an elevation
  • Round sections inferred where the views agree
  • An STL you can open in other tools

Checked against them

  • The solid projected back onto every view
  • Conflicts between views marked in red
  • Rebuilds in milliseconds as you edit
  • Runs in your browser, with nothing uploaded
