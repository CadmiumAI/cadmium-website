What is happening

From pixels to something you can query.

Clean

Threshold between paper and ink, measure the skew from how sharply the pipework lines up, then remove it.

Find

Closed shapes become equipment and instruments. Long straight strokes become pipe. What remains is matched to a symbol library.

Read

Tags are read glyph by glyph and decoded as whole words against the tag grammar, which settles what shape alone cannot.

Connect

Touching strokes join into lines, and lines into a graph of what connects to what. Isolation is then a search on that graph.

What you get

A drawing you can query, not just view.

Read from the page

  • Symbols, equipment and instruments
  • Tags and line numbers
  • A score for every read
  • Export to CSV

Answers from the graph

  • What connects to what, line by line
  • Which valves isolate any item
  • Dangling line ends
  • Off-sheet connections

Isolation results are for engineer review. They are not a safety procedure and must not be used as one.
