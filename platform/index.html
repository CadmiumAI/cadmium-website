Platform

Every engineering document, as one dataset.

Cadmium reads the documents your project already produces, links them through the tags, lines and geometry they share, and writes finished deliverables back out.

How it fits together

Read, connect, generate, check.

Read

3D models, P&IDs and scanned drawings become structured data, with a link back to the source.

Connect

Tags, lines and geometry join into one graph of the plant, across documents and revisions.

Generate

Isometrics, MTOs, registers and models are produced on your templates and standards.

Check

Every output is verified against its sources, and conflicts go to an engineer for review.
