Questions security teams ask

Common questions.

Is our data used to train models?

No. Customer drawings and models are never used to train models shared with other customers.

Where is our data stored?

In the region you choose, or entirely inside your own environment. Data never leaves it without your approval.

Can it run without internet access?

Yes. The on-premises edition runs on air-gapped networks, with updates delivered as signed packages.

How do we delete a project?

Project owners can delete a project at any time. Data is purged from storage and backups within 30 days.

Can we restrict access by project?

Yes. Access is role-based and set per project, including for joint-venture partners and subcontractors.

Will you complete our security questionnaire?

Yes. We provide our SOC 2 report under NDA and respond to vendor security reviews.
